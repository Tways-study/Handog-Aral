# Changelog

Version history for Handog Aral, reconstructed from the git log. v2 was the Vite app; v3 is the Next.js overhaul, merged to `main` (see `docs/v3-plan.md` for status). `package.json` is `3.0.0-beta.1`.

## v3 (3.0.0-beta.1)

### 2026-10-24 · Hardening
- **Unpunctuated pages lost their AI explanation:** a scanned list, poem or heading has no sentence end, so the "sentence" sent with a tapped word could be the whole page and the proxy refused it (limit 600). Context is now a window of at most 400 characters around the word.
- A whole book pasted or scanned is cut to one page (6,000 characters, at a sentence end) with a note, instead of thousands of word buttons.
- Words typed or scanned with a combining accent (n + tilde) stay one word (NFC; marks allowed by the proxy schema).
- OCR debris (over 64 letters) is no longer sent, cached or queued for retries that could never succeed.
- Child names are clipped to 30 characters everywhere (settings v5, legacy import).
- Checked and fine: 320px with Large text, the dyslexia font and an emoji name on every screen; 4,000 saved words list in under a second.

### 2026-10-24 · Owl: bundled first-visit still
- A pre-rendered idle 3D owl (11 KB, `src/lib/owl/bundledPoster.ts`, generated by `scripts/gen-owl-poster.mjs`, loaded as its own tiny chunk only by owls that may go 3D) is shown on a device's very first visit, so even then the flat SVG is visible for about 50 ms instead of about 4 s. Regenerate it, and bump `POSTER_VERSION`, whenever the 3D model, palette or camera changes.

### 2026-10-24 · Owl no longer flashes the old model
- Every page mounts its own owl, which started as the flat SVG and swapped to the 3D owl a moment later, so it looked like it kept switching back to the old model. The 3D owl now saves a still of itself (`src/lib/owl/poster.ts`, kept in memory and localStorage, versioned) that stands in from the first paint of later pages and later visits; the live canvas fades in over identical pixels and the still stays until it is fully opaque. Only shown where 3D is allowed; the first ever visit still shows the SVG once.

### 2026-10-24 · Premium feel (branch `premium-apple-feel`)
- Frosted chrome (`.material`): bottom nav, desktop rail, reader bar and word sheet are translucent glass with a bright top edge. It falls back to solid paper for reduced transparency, increased contrast, forced colors, low-end or save-data devices and "Gentle animations" off.
- Springs: presses answer at once (scale .97) and release on a damped spring (`--ease-spring`, a CSS `linear()` curve); the switch thumb uses the same spring; dialogs arrive critically damped (no bounce, as nothing flicked them). Reduced motion keeps short ease-outs.
- Optical type: h1 tightened to -0.02em, h2 -0.01em (OpenDyslexic keeps its own spacing); squircle corners on buttons, tiles and the sheet where supported; the primary CTA gets a light top edge.

### 2026-10-24 · Polish pass
- Horizon props shrink on phones so they never sit behind a long subtitle; page titles get breathing room below the band.
- The owl blinks eight times after it appears (CSS, same gate as the scenery); Settings: "Gentle animations".
- Scan gets the same title band as every other screen; Home's phone cover tucks the small book behind the label card; the Home empty state no longer repeats a heading.
- Removed the wavy underline from section headings.
- e2e: replaced stale Hiligaynon literals and dismissed the first-visit sticker dialog in the streak test.

### 2026-10-24 · Barrio horizon band
- The page band's confetti is replaced by a small barrio horizon (mountains, a nipa hut on terraced hills, sun, clouds; the page ground is the front hill). It rises into place once, the clouds drift for about two minutes and settle. Pure CSS, no JS, transform/opacity only; still under reduced motion, save-data, low-end devices or the new Settings "Moving scenery" switch; hidden under forced colors; a dark-mode night twin with a moon.

### 2026-10-23 · Hiligaynon removed, English and Filipino both ways
- Hiligaynon is gone (translations were not accurate enough). The language setting is now Filipino or English and doubles as the lookup direction: English words explained in Filipino, or Filipino words explained in English.
- Four short Filipino stories and a ~200-word curated Filipino dictionary (marked for native-speaker review), a Filipino TTS voice, and an AI prompt that switches direction.
- Existing installs: stored language becomes Filipino; saved Hiligaynon words are kept and upgraded in the background; Hiligaynon cache and queue rows are dropped (Dexie v2, settings v4).

### 2026-10-22 · Fun and cute round
- Procedural three.js toon owl (lazy, with a flat SVG fallback and safe refusals on slow, low-end or reduced-motion devices), moods and a wardrobe (glasses, cap, bow).
- Rewards: 14 stickers, celebration dialog, "Pares" memory game, optional synthesized sounds and haptics, first-time moments.
- Redrawn art, shape-coded difficulty, GSAP motion gated on reduced motion.
- Fixes: offline crash from lazy chunks (`softDynamic`), settings mirror no longer rehydrates from an older copy.

### 2026-10-21 · Polish, production hardening, accessibility
- Pad Paper Storybook comp approved; `DESIGN.md` written from the finished build and the notebook label colours moved into tokens.
- Review fixes: authored rubber-stamp star and check, neutral disabled buttons, left-aligned Quiz and Settings columns, Home tally of two chips, a single percentage, taller cover and pad on desktop.
- Polish: tactile pressed states, notebook Welcome, Home shelf, Books counts, calmer Settings, scan tiles and processing moment, segmented Words filter with skeletons, quiz results stamps, empty progress at 0%.
- Production: in-world error pages, metadata and social previews, security headers, install button, manifest shortcuts and install screenshots, release checklist (`docs/release-checklist.md`).
- Performance (revertible): synchronous `localStorage` mirror for the stores and a deferred data-layer boot.
- Accessibility: Playwright axe suite for WCAG 2.1 AA; accessible names now come from visible text (WCAG 2.5.3); bottom nav labels survive Large font, dyslexia font and Tagalog.
- Rewrote `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` for the v3 architecture.

### 2026-10-19 · Phases 2 to 4: data layer, design system, screens
- **Data layer:** Dexie database, persisted zustand stores, one-time import of the pre-v3 `localStorage` blob, word-explanation pipeline, offline lookup queue, tokenizer.
- **AI proxy:** `/api/explain`, `/api/ocr` and `/api/health` routes with a server-side Gemini module, per-IP Upstash rate limiting, origin allowlist and zod contracts. The Gemini key is no longer in the client.
- **Offline OCR and TTS:** self-hosted Tesseract assets and a text-to-speech wrapper (native on Android, Web Speech elsewhere).
- **Design system:** pad-paper tokens with a WCAG contrast test, shadcn components adapted to them, `DifficultyStamp`, GSAP motion helpers gated on `prefers-reduced-motion`, SVG owl mascot and book covers, dev-only `/design` showcase.
- **App shell and PWA:** bottom nav and left rail, `PageHeader`/`PageContainer`, theme sync, offline banner, manifest and icons, prompt-to-update service worker.
- **Screens:** Home, Books, Welcome, Reader (`/read/[bookId]`), Scan, word panel, Words, Quiz and Settings, with Hiligaynon and Tagalog strings split per feature.
- Removed the legacy `AppContext`, Gemini client, old services and legacy CSS.
- Fixed: keyboard focus ring (Tailwind v4 `outline-none` vs `focus-visible:outline-3`), focus moved into and trapped in the word drawer, the service worker no longer caches `/api/*`, precache glob follows `NEXT_DIST_DIR`.
- Added Playwright e2e specs for first run, reader, quiz, settings, offline lookup and upgrade, scan, reduced motion and keyboard use.
